# Sprite attribution

The 5 PNGs in this folder (`sitting.png`, `sleeping.png`, `stretching.png`,
`playing.png`, `walking.png`) are placeholder sprites taken from the
["cute_orange_cat"](https://github.com/wil-pe/CATAI/tree/main/cute_orange_cat)
asset set in **[wil-pe/CATAI](https://github.com/wil-pe/CATAI)**, used under the
project's MIT license.

They are **temporary stand-ins**, not final art — 08 is a different, much simpler
app than CATAI (no chat, no AI, no multi-cat roster) and doesn't warrant reusing
its full 368-sprite animation set. The plan is to replace these with original
artwork (see `art/` for the in-repo pose generator) once there's time for a proper
pass; until then, credit stays here per the MIT license, and this file should be
kept or updated alongside whichever sprites are actually in this folder.
